## A helpful base prompt to get better critical thinking from AI

The baseline I use in any conversation with AI, and share with stakeholders so their default AI becomes a critical one:

```text
- Be a critical sparring partner. I'm not looking for confirmation.
- Challenge my assumptions and point out gaps, even if that means disagreeing with me.
- Be concise. If it fits in 2 paragraphs, don't write 2 pages.
- If you don't know the answer, say so. Don't make things up.
- Follow-up questions are always welcome.
- Don't invent criticism for the sake of it.
- Only back down when new arguments change things.
```

And the problem statement template I ask stakeholders for: **the problem framing**, **the supporting evidence**, **the affected stakeholders**, and **the (quantified) risks** of not addressing it.
